01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

From paper records to an accountable digital workflow.

The concept is designed around the realities of station operations: capture quickly, find information immediately, assign responsibility and preserve the record.

01

Capture once

Structured forms reduce incomplete entries and make key details easier to review.

02

Find instantly

Search OB numbers, people, dates, categories and other indexed fields from one register.

03

Follow the action

Assignments, actions, amendments and supervisory decisions remain visible in context.

02 / WORKFLOW

A simple chain of custody for every occurrence.

01Record

Officer captures the occurrence and supporting identification details.

02Assign

Supervisor assigns follow-up responsibility and status.

03Act

Actions are added without losing the history of the record.

04Review

Supervisor reviews, amends where necessary and closes the matter.

03 / SECURITY PRINCIPLES

Built to move toward secure, auditable deployment.

The prototype separates authentication, role access, sensitive-field protection and audit history so the architecture can mature into a properly governed operational system.

01Strong password hashing with a server-side pepper
02Encrypted identification fields at rest
03Role and station-level access controls
04Version history and audit events
